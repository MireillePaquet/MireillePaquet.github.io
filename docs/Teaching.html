Teaching and supervision

Prospective students and postdoctoral fellows

I supervise doctoral and M.A. students working on immigration and public policy, from Canadian and comparative perspectives. The Department of Political Science at Concordia offers immigration-specific courses, and students join an active research community through the Institute for Research on Migration and Society (IRMS) and the Bridging Divides research program.

I also welcome inquiries from prospective postdoctoral fellows, including applicants to SSHRC, FRQSC and international competitions.

If you are interested in working with me, please write to mireille.paquet [at] concordia.ca with:

  • a short description of your research interests (one page is enough)
  • your CV and transcripts
  • a writing sample

En français. Je supervise des étudiantes et étudiants à la maîtrise et au doctorat, ainsi que des stagiaires postdoctoraux, qui souhaitent travailler en français. À Concordia, il est possible de rédiger son mémoire ou sa thèse en français. N’hésitez pas à m’écrire pour discuter de vos intérêts de recherche et des possibilités de financement.

Supervision

I currently supervise students, all funded by SSHRC, as well as M.A. students and three postdoctoral fellows. Former postdoctoral fellows I supervised now hold faculty positions at Concordia University, the Université de Montréal and Roskilde University; others work in research and in the public service.

I strongly believe that people with doctoral training are needed in all areas of society. I support my students in whichever path they choose, and I welcome applicants who already know they want to work outside academia.

Courses

I teach the following courses on a rotating basis, in Political Science and as part of Concordia’s Migration Studies Certificate.

Undergraduate

  • POLI 316 / SCPA 351: Immigration in Quebec and Canada
  • POLI 328: Public Policy and the Politics of Equality
  • POLI 341: Provincial and Territorial Politics
  • POLI 353: Principles of Public Policy
  • POLI 489: Immigration, Integration and the State

Graduate

  • POLI 636 / 805: Theories of Public Policy and Public Administration
  • POLI 683 / 815: The State and Immigration

I also supervise honours theses (POLI 396) and internships (POLI 497).
